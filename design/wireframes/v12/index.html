Pulse — app design, v12

B's measured column and card treatment as the base, A's instrument density on the analytics screen. Real numbers from the local database. Every screen holds at 390, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 with no horizontal scroll, and every text colour is at or above 4.5:1 on the surface it sits on.

Screens

AnalyticsA's density inside B's column. The pointer reads the series against the previous week; picking a row scopes the page to it.
SitesCards answer "is it healthy and busy" — a week of shape, not a tracking id. The teaching empty state is shown below the cards so you can see both; in the app it is one or the other.
SetupLeads with whether it worked. The waiting strip becomes the dashboard when the first event lands.
SettingsTwo ordinary cards and one that is not, marked by its own edge rather than a red heading alone.
Ask AIThe query sits under the answer rather than behind a disclosure — showing the SQL is the feature's whole claim.
Add siteTwo fields, with the snippet they are about to get shown beside them.
AccountPassword rules resolve as you type instead of failing after submit.
